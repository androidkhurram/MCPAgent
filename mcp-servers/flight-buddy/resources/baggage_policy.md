# Flight Buddy — baggage notes (sample)

Use this as read-only context for the agent. Always verify with the airline before travel.

## Typical economy allowances (indicative)

- **Saudia (SV):** often includes checked bag on international economy; confirm weight (commonly ~23 kg).
- **Airblue (PA):** policies vary by fare; many international tickets include checked baggage — confirm at booking.
- **Turkish Airlines (TK):** international economy usually includes checked baggage; weight depends on route/fare.
- **Emirates (EK):** checked bag commonly included on international economy.

## Tips for the agent

- Prefer stating “confirm on the airline site” rather than guaranteeing free bags.
- Mention carry-on limits separately from checked bags when known.
- If SerpApi extensions mention “Checked baggage for a fee”, surface that clearly.
